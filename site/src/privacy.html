What the game stores on your device

To remember your choices, the game saves four small values in storage on your own device:

  • your best score,
  • whether sound is on,

These values never leave your device and we cannot see them. A game in progress is not saved. Uninstalling the app removes these values. In the web version, clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.
