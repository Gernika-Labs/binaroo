A puzzle game

{{name}}: learn binary by playing.

Fill the board so every row adds up to its target number. Each cell is a bit, each row is a byte. Match the number before the row's timer runs out.

Play in your browser iPhone: coming soon Android: coming soon

How it plays

1

Pick a shape

16 shapes, from a single square to big L pieces. Harder shapes score more points.

2

Make the bits add up

The columns are worth 128, 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2 and 1. Fill the right cells so the row's total equals its target.

3

Beat the clock

Match exactly before the row's timer ends. Five exact rows take you to the next level, and the clock gets shorter.

What you pick up

You practise place values and powers of two, converting between decimal and binary, and thinking in bytes, without being told to memorise anything. A wrong bit marks a row as impossible, so you learn why each bit matters.
