How to play

How does a row clear?

A row clears only when its cells add up exactly to the target number shown on the right. The columns are worth 128, 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2 and 1, left to right. A filled cell shows 1 and an empty cell shows 0. Filling a whole row that does not match does not clear it.

What does "impossible" mean?

A row is impossible when it has a filled cell that its target number doesn't include. Cells can't be removed, so that row can no longer match. It stays marked until its timer runs out, when it gets a new target.

What are the timers and strikes?

Every row has its own countdown. When a row runs out of time you lose 25 points, get a strike, and the row gets a fresh target. Three strikes end the game.

How do levels and scoring work?

Five exact rows move you up a level. Higher levels have shorter timers and targets with more bits set. You earn points for each shape you place (harder shapes pay more), plus a bonus for each exact row that grows with the time you had left, and a streak bonus for consecutive exact rows.

What is the hint?

If you wait about 10 seconds without a move, the game highlights the reachable row closest to its target. A hint costs 15 points.

Your data

How do I reset my best score?

In the app, uninstall and reinstall it. In the browser, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Do you collect my data?
